## Document Description (from known metadata)

This is Mikhail Ershov's survey on the Golod-Shafarevich theorem and its applications. It is a comprehensive survey covering:

- The original Golod-Shafarevich inequality and proof
- Applications to the class field tower problem
- Pro-p group theory and presentations
- The role of the Golod-Shafarevich inequality in group theory
- Extensions and generalizations of the theorem
- Applications in various areas of algebra and number theory

## Relevance to This Project

The Golod-Shafarevich theorem is central to the construction of infinite p-class field towers used in the Erdős unit distance problem. Specifically:
- The criterion d² > 4r (where d = number of generators, r = number of relations) guarantees infinite class field towers
- The tower construction requires fields with many ramified primes to ensure sufficiently many generators in the 2-cohomology of the Galois group
