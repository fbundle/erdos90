*   **Goal**: Produce an infinite sequence of number fields with bounded root discriminant and specific splitting behavior.
*   **Mathematical Prerequisites**:

### Postulate 2: CM Class-Group Data (`exists_cm_class_group_data`)
*   **Goal**: Find many norm-one elements in a lattice with controlled class group size.
*   **Mathematical Prerequisites**:
    *   **CM Field Theory**: Splitting of primes in $K/F$. Specifically, if $q$ splits in $F$ and $q \equiv 1 \pmod 4$, then $q$ splits in $F(i)$.
    *   **Relative Class Group Bounds**: Louboutin's bound $h^-(K) \leq C^d \cdot \text{rd}(K/F)^d$ or Minkowski-based bounds.
    *   **Principal Ideal Generator Choice**: Constructing $\alpha \in K^\times$ such that $(\alpha) = \mathfrak{A}_1 \mathfrak{A}_2^{-1}$ and using $\alpha/c(\alpha)$ as a norm-one element.
