## Abstract (from arXiv:1901.04354)

Given a prime p, a number field K and a finite set of places S of K, let K_S be the maximal pro-p extension of K unramified outside S. The researchers employ the Golod-Shafarevich criterion to demonstrate the existence of infinite subextensions of K_S with bounded ramification in both tame and wild cases. They achieve new records on Martinet constants in totally real and totally complex cases, and answer a question of Ihara by producing infinite asymptotically good extensions in which infinitely many primes split completely.

## Context

Both papers are cited as reference [20] and/or [21] in arXiv:2605.20695 (Alon et al., "Remarks on the disproof of the unit distance conjecture"):

> [20] Farshid Hajir, Christian Maire, and Ravi Ramakrishna, Cutting towers of number fields, Annales Mathématiques du Québec 45 (2021), 321–345.

The Hajir-Maire-Ramakrishna work provides the infinite class field tower construction with bounded root discriminant that is essential for the unit distance proof. Their key contribution is showing that one can construct towers K₀ ⊂ K₁ ⊂ K₂ ⊂ ... of CM fields with:
- [Kₙ : Q] → ∞
- rdiscr(Kₙ) = |disc(Kₙ)|^(1/[Kₙ:Q]) bounded (by a constant depending only on the base field)
- Specific rational primes split completely in all Kₙ (the "good" primes that give norm-1 elements)

This bounded root discriminant property, combined with the Minkowski bound on class numbers, is what makes the overall construction work.
