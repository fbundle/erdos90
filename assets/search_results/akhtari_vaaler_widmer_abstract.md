## Title
Effective equidistribution of norm one elements in CM-fields

## Abstract

The paper examines a mathematical group denoted $\mathcal{S}_K$ representing "the maximal subgroup of the multiplicative group $K^\times$ that embeds into the unit circle under each embedding of $K$ into the complex numbers." The authors characterize this group's behavior in two scenarios: when $K=\mathbb{Q}(\mathcal{S}_K)$ is a CM-field versus when it is not. They establish that in the CM-field case, $\mathcal{S}_K$ functions as the kernel of a relative norm map from $K^\times$ to the multiplicative subgroup of the maximal totally real subfield.
