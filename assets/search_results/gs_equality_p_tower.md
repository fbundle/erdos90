# A Golod-Shafarevich Equality and p-Tower Groups

## Abstract
The paper addresses techniques for demonstrating that number fields possess infinite p-class field towers, which depend on various formulations of the Golod-Shafarevich inequality. The research restricts the types of p-groups functioning as Galois groups in finite p-class field towers. For quadratic imaginary number fields, the theory demonstrates that finite such groups must belong to one of three presentation types. The author proves a Golod-Shafarevich equality for analytic pro-p-groups by tracking error terms in standard proofs. As application, the work extends Skopin's findings, showing that groups of the third type are "necessarily tremendously large."
